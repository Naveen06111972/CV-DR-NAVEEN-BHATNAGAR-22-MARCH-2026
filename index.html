Professional Summary

Prof. (Dr.) Naveen Bhatnagar is a distinguished academician with over 25 years of experience in Industry & Education. He has worked as Associate Dean SOET, Associate Director DCPD at Chandigarh University, and Director/Manager across various Universities, Colleges and Corporate organisations. Associated with AIMA as Expert/Observer/SME, SPJIMR as Expert Panel member since May 2023, and NIRA (No Droit Indian Robotics Assistant, Bangalore) as expert panel member.

Presently associated with Torah Learning Solutions as Senior Manager Training, imparting Trainings/Placement in Galgotia University, Greater Noida since September 2025. Core expertise spans AI/ML/Deep Learning, Business Analytics, Campus Recruitment Training, Curriculum Development, and Institutional Building.
